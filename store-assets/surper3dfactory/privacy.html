Privacy Policy

Super 3D Modeling Factory · 超级3D建模工厂
Developer: hao wang · Updated September 8, 2026
Applies to the iOS app.

Data handled by the app

The app does not require an account and does not send project files, personal information, usage analytics or advertising identifiers to the developer. It does not include advertising or tracking services. Your project library and current-project selection are stored on your device.

Import and export

The app reads only files you select in the system file picker. Export creates a temporary copy and opens the iOS Files destination picker; the temporary export copy is removed when you finish or cancel the picker. Files saved to your chosen destination remain there.

If you choose iCloud Drive or another file provider, that provider may transfer or store the file under its own policy. Device backups may also include app data depending on your system settings. The app does not operate its own cloud synchronization service.

Your choices and retention

You can delete individual projects or clear the local library in the app. Exported copies and device/cloud backups must be managed separately. Removing the app normally removes its local container; restored backups may restore app data. Keep your own copies of important projects.

Permissions and third-party components

File access is initiated by your import or export action. The app uses Flutter and standard libraries for local storage and file selection. It does not request camera, microphone, contacts or location access for its core functions.

Support and this website

If you email support, your email address and the message you choose to send are used to respond to your request. Please do not send passwords or confidential project files. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages; the hosting service may process connection information such as IP addresses in order to serve pages. Opening website links uses your browser and network connection.

Children and changes

The app does not knowingly collect children's personal data and is not specifically directed at children. Changes to this policy will be published here with a revised date.
