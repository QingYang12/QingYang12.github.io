FoxAI: Unified AI Workspace — Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 18, 2026 · Version 2.3

Our commitment

FoxAI: Unified AI Workspace is a browser extension that provides unified access to multiple AI tools (chat, podcast generation, image editing, video workflows, and 3D content creation) through a side panel interface. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

Data Collection

We do not collect any personal data. All interactions with AI services happen directly between your browser and the AI providers you configure. No data passes through our servers.

  • No user accounts or registration required
  • No analytics or tracking scripts
  • No telemetry or usage statistics
  • No cookies or local storage beyond what Chrome requires for extension settings

Permissions Explained

This extension requests the following permissions:

  • sidePanel — Display the FoxAI workspace in Chrome's side panel
  • storage — Save your API configuration and preferences locally
  • tabs, activeTab — Allow interaction with web pages for content extraction or sharing
  • <all_urls> — Enable the extension to work on any webpage you visit

All permissions are used solely for core functionality. No data is sent to third parties.

API Keys & Credentials

If you configure external AI services (OpenAI, Claude, etc.), your API keys are stored locally in Chrome's extension storage. They are never transmitted to our servers or any third party except the respective AI provider when you make a request.

Content You Generate

All content you create through FoxAI (chat messages, generated images, podcasts, 3D models) remains entirely under your control. We have no access to this content unless you explicitly choose to share it.

Third-Party Services

FoxAI integrates with external AI providers. When you use these services, their respective privacy policies apply to the data you send them. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of any AI service you connect to FoxAI.

Children's Privacy

This extension does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted here with an updated effective date.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through the Chrome Web Store developer page.
